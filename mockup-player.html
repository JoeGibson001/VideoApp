Verdict: video player mockup

Interactive. Drag with the mouse or touch, or use the keyboard.

Gestures

  • Drag right past 30% = Like, left = Dislike (auto-advance)
  • Drag up = next video, down = previous (no rating)
  • Tap video = play / pause
  • Heart = Favorite, stays on this video
  • Chevron next to tags = details sheet

Keyboard

  • Left / Right = rate, Up / Down = navigate, Space = pause, F = favorite, I = details

Notes

  • Video 2 is landscape to show letterboxing (contain fit, no stretch)
  • Video 3 has no audio, so the mute button shows "No sound"
  • Coloured blocks stand in for real video
